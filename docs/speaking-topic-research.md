## Duration and selection

[Amazon India guidance](https://prod.contactus.jobsatamazon.hvh.a2z.com/india/in-cs-vcs-job-assessment-en) describes language proficiency and authentic responses, but does not publish a fixed speaking topic bank or universal 2–3 minute requirement. Accounts vary; Suzie discusses shorter responses around 14:15, while the written VCS account says no strict limit was specified. The app offers 1, 2 and 3 minute practice limits without presenting them as policy.
